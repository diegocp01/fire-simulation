# Fire Simulation

![A wildfire lights up a mountain ridge beneath a smoky night sky.](https://github.com/diegocp01/fire-simulation/releases/download/preview/fire-simulation.png)

An interactive view of a mountain wildfire at night, generated with **Claude Fable 5.2 Max**. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser. Drag to look around and scroll or pinch to zoom.

## Original prompt

> Create a cinematic, photorealistic view of a wildfire burning along a mountain ridge at night, seen from the valley floor, in a self-contained HTML file. Generate the scene entirely through code, without downloaded images or 3D models. The camera drifts slowly through the valley, looking up at the ridge. Make the camera interactive: click and drag to look around a full 360 degrees, scroll to zoom in and out, and resume the automatic drift after a few seconds of no input. Support touch too (drag to look, pinch to zoom). Choose the techniques yourself, then render, inspect, and iteratively improve the flames and their flicker, embers rising on the wind, smoke columns lit from below, the orange glow on the horizon and surrounding terrain, silhouetted trees, falling ash, and the camera movement until they feel convincing.
